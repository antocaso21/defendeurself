What the project does

From evidence to confident digital behaviour

DefendEUrself combines cross-national research, accessible training and a European support network to strengthen cybersecurity competence among adults aged 45 and above.

Evidence-Based Research

Understanding the real cybersecurity experiences, vulnerabilities and learning needs of older adults across five European countries.

Accessible Cybersecurity Training

Interactive Micro-MOOCs built around recognisable situations, clear language, repetition and practical response scripts.

Digital Resilience and Community

Peer learning, intergenerational cooperation and a multilingual network that supports safer participation in digital life.

Project overview

Older adults are online. The missing piece is protection.

DefendEUrself is an Erasmus+ KA220-ADU partnership led by Open Education Hub and involving organisations from Belgium, Greece, Italy, Lithuania and Portugal. The project responds to a central challenge: essential services have moved online faster than cybersecurity competence has developed.

The project turns awareness into practical, repeatable and confidence-building behaviour through research, training, community engagement and policy dialogue.
